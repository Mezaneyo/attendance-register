What it does
  • Create classes or groups and add their members (one per line)
  • Take attendance for each session: Present, Late, Absent or Excused
  • See each member's attendance %, with members below the minimum shown in red
  • Edit or delete members and sessions
  • Export the report to Excel, PDF, Word or CSV
How attendance % works

Attendance % = (Present + Late) ÷ (Present + Late + Absent) × 100. Excused sessions are left out of the calculation. Sessions where a member was not marked are also left out.
